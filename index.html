Setup Guide

Get Ready

💻 Laptop / Desktop

Place your laptop on a table at chest height. Step back 6–8 feet until your entire upper body is clearly visible in the frame.

📱 Mobile / Tablet

Prop your phone against a wall or stable surface at waist height. Step back 8–10 feet until your entire body is clearly visible in the frame.

💡

Make sure the room is well-lit — face a window or lamp for best tracking accuracy.

🎯

Keep your full upper body centered in the frame for punch detection.

🔒

Your camera feed is processed entirely on your device. Nothing is uploaded or stored.
