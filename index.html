Setup Guide

Get Ready

💻 Laptop / Desktop

Place your laptop on a table at chest height. Step back 6–8 ft so your full upper body is clearly in frame.

📱 Mobile / Tablet

Prop your phone against a wall at waist height. Step back 8–10 ft so your full body fits in frame.

💡

Face a window or lamp — good lighting dramatically improves tracking accuracy.

🎯

Keep your full upper body centered so both wrists stay visible at all times.

🔒

All AI processing happens locally on your device. Nothing is uploaded or stored.

📷
